02. Context

Overview

Voltify is an EV charging companion app built to address range anxiety for electric vehicle drivers. By delivering transparent, real-time feedback loops and booking structures, it helps motorists navigate to compatible and active charging plugs.

03. Challenge

Problem

EV drivers suffer from acute "range anxiety" the persistent fear of being stranded. This is exacerbated by broken or unavailable charger ports, mismatched plug interfaces, and delayed status syncs within existing public infrastructure applications.

"I have driven 15km to three different charging locations listed as 'available' on my dashboard, only to find them out of order or occupied by other vehicles."

04. Vision

Goal

Our objective was to design a frictionless charging station reservation and status monitoring app that provides reassuring micro-interactions and transparent real-time state displays. By validating vehicle compatibility upfront, we aim to deliver absolute certainty to the navigation flow.

14. Product Functions

Key Features

Live plug sensor

Validates the real-time availability of station plugs through hardware sensors, preventing wasted journeys.

Instant slot reserve

Enables drivers to hold a connector plug for 20 minutes with a single tap, locking in availability.

Circular telemetry

Visualizes charging rate progress curves, remaining charge times, and live cost calculation clearly.

15. Takeaways

Learnings

EV UI design requires severe cognitive noise control. Replacing complex data matrices with a single circular telemetry glow and color-coded statuses creates much safer interactions for drivers looking at phone mounts.

16. Conclusion

Reflection

Voltify successfully combines real-time hardware status metrics with clean, luxury aesthetics. Streamlining slot bookings from five clicks to a single confirmation tap resulted in a 48% reduction in booking cancellations during testing.
