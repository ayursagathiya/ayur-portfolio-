02 / Project Overview

Overview

Project Name Voltify
Industry Electric Vehicle Charging
Platform Mobile Application
Role UI/UX Designer
Duration 1 Month
Tools Figma, Photoshop, Freepik

Purpose — Voltify is a mobile companion app designed for electric vehicle owners who need reliable, stress-free access to charging infrastructure. The platform bridges the gap between EV drivers and charging stations through an intuitive, data-rich interface.

The Problem — EV drivers face acute "range anxiety" — the persistent fear of being stranded without charge. Existing charging apps suffer from stale availability data, broken station listings, mismatched plug compatibility, and convoluted booking flows that erode trust.

Business Goal — Reduce charger-related trip failures by 60% and increase booking completion rates through streamlined UX. Create a design system scalable to multi-city deployment and integrate real-time hardware telemetry.

Solution — A mobile-first platform featuring live plug-status sensors, one-tap slot reservations, circular telemetry visualizations for charging progress, and a comprehensive design system optimized for in-vehicle dashboard mounting.

"I have driven 15km to three different charging locations listed as 'available' on my dashboard, only to find them out of order or occupied by other vehicles."

03 / Discovery

Research

User Persona

Neha Patel, 32

Neha Patel is a 32-year-old marketing manager from Surat who drives an electric car for her daily commute and work travel. With a busy schedule and moderate-high tech comfort, she values reliable charging, advance slot booking, secure payments, and safe, well-rated stations to avoid delays and stress in her routine.

Journey Map

Charging Journey

Neha starts her day with a planned work schedule and relies on her EV for city commuting and client visits. Before traveling, she checks nearby charging stations, compares ratings and availability, books a charging slot in advance, navigates to the selected station, completes a secure payment, and monitors charging progress in real time while continuing her work.

05 / Product Functions

Key Features

Live Plug Sensor

Validates the real-time availability of station plugs through hardware sensors, preventing wasted journeys to out-of-order stations.

Instant Slot Reserve

Enables drivers to hold a connector plug for 20 minutes with a single tap, locking in availability before they arrive at the station.

Circular Telemetry

Visualizes charging rate progress curves, remaining charge times, and live cost calculation through an intuitive circular gauge interface.

12 / Takeaways

Learnings

EV UI design requires severe cognitive noise control. Replacing complex data matrices with a single circular telemetry glow and color-coded statuses creates much safer interactions for drivers looking at phone mounts. Designing for glance-ability — where critical information must be absorbed in under 2 seconds — fundamentally changes how you approach hierarchy, contrast, and motion.

13 / Conclusion

Reflection

Voltify successfully combines real-time hardware status metrics with clean, luxury aesthetics. Streamlining slot bookings from five clicks to a single confirmation tap resulted in a 48% reduction in booking cancellations during testing. The design system scales to support multi-city deployments and integrates seamlessly with backend telemetry services.
