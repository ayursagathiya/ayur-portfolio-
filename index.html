WHO I AM

Designing experiences, not just screens.

I'm Ayur Sagathiya — a digital product designer with a passion for crafting interfaces that feel intuitive, purposeful, and alive. Currently pursuing my MCA, I channel every pixel into meaningful user experiences.

My design philosophy blends product thinking with deep user research. I don't just make things look good — I make them work beautifully. Every decision is backed by empathy, data, and a relentless focus on the end user.

From mobile banking interfaces to design systems, I approach each project as a holistic experience — understanding context, mapping user flows, and iterating toward clarity.

When I'm not designing, I'm exploring interaction design patterns, studying motion principles, and pushing the boundaries of what a digital product can feel like.

06 — DESIGN PROCESS

DESIGN PROCESS

A highly analytical, step-by-step methodology from research to launch.

01

Research & Empathize

Deep-diving into user demographics, conducting interviews, and analyzing competitor friction points.

02

Define Problem

Synthesizing raw research into user personas, core HMW (How Might We) questions, and project scoping parameters.

03

Ideate & Brainstorm

Sketching initial solutions, mapping conceptual flows, and reviewing architectural options for clarity.

04

Wireframing

Creating low-fidelity structures to establish structural layouts and validate path efficiency.

05

High-Fidelity UI

Designing beautiful visual states, locking in typographic hierarchies, and crafting fluid micro-interactions.

06

User Testing

Conducting interactive walkthroughs, noting user friction, and measuring task success rates.

07

Iterate & Handover

Polishing components based on usability feedback, preparing redlines, exporting assets, and aligning design tokens for developer handoff.
