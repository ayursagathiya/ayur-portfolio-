02 / CASE STUDY

NeroBean Experience

Designing a sensory, luxury technology-inspired web presence for an elite small-batch coffee roasting brand.

ROLE

Creative Technologist

TIMELINE

4 Weeks (2025)

DELIVERABLES

Brand Identity, Landing Layout

DESIGN STYLE

Luxury Minimalist tech

The Challenge

Standard coffee shop landing pages are generic: full of stock images of baristas smiling, cluttered menus, and boring templates.

NeroBean roasted beans are curated for software developers, UI designers, and creative technologist profiles. The interface needed to feel as sophisticated as a high-end SaaS product (like Linear or Stripe) while conveying organic, warm sensory delight.

CORE STRATEGY

Using strict grid alignments, gold radial gradients, elegant type sizing contrast, and interactive 3D elements to establish a premium luxury digital cafe storefront.

Visual Rationale

The Color Psychology

We defined a warm neutral dark-mode layout: deep obsidian backgrounds (`#090605`) combined with rich roast golds (`#C58F67`) and milky whites (`#F5E6D3`). This reminds users of an authentic espresso bar during the golden hour, while keeping screen contrast perfect for nighttime coders.

Satoshi Display Typography

We chose the geometric, wide-aligned typeface **Satoshi** paired with **Inter**. Satoshi's bold cuts and tight letter tracking reflect modern technological precision, transforming basic coffee titles into visual structures.

Interactive High-Fidelity Design

Kinetic Mechanics

When scroll triggers initiate, coffee beans scatter across the screen in parallax depth. Hovering over coffee varieties dynamically lights up card outlines using mouse coordinates inside our custom LERP cursor shader.

Lessons Learned

Aesthetics are a core component of digital brand trust. By applying clean grid layout mathematics (typically seen in fintech products) to an organic retail project, we created a distinctive, premium positioning that immediately stands out from competitors.
