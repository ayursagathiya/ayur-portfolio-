05 / CASE STUDY

AirPods Max Landing

Crafting an editorial, high-end sensory product showcase detailing premium acoustic engineering and active noise cancellation.

ROLE

UI/UX & Motion Designer

TIMELINE

3 Weeks (2025)

DELIVERABLES

High-Fidelity Web Layout, Scroll Motion Rationale

TECH STACK

Figma, GSAP Engine

The Concept

Standard product pages present headphones as basic list features with stale specs, failing to convey the luxury materials and sensory soundscape details.

We designed an experiential, narrative-driven presentation for the AirPods Max. By leveraging editorial layout, large typographic cuts, and immersive motion design, we created a digital experience that mirrors Apple's premium physical detailing.

DESIGN STRATEGY

Using strict minimalist structures, rich radial gradients, and fluid scroll-based transitions to communicate materials and spatial audio dynamics.

Visual & Motion Rationale

Acoustic Visuals

Sound is invisible, making it difficult to market online. We designed a series of structural diagrams mapping the canopy, ear cushions, and telescoping arms. As the user scrolls, components animate into place, illustrating the physical tension and seal that creates superior active noise isolation.

The Scroll Sequence

1. Hero State — Exploded view of AirPods Max floating on canvas.
2. Mesh Detail — Parallax zoom into the knit mesh headband material.
3. Acoustics — Audio wave frequencies animate as noise cancellation layers slide in.
4. Colorways — Interactive ring of available aluminum finishes.

Key Takeaway

Premium products require premium digital storytelling. Standard grid layouts can convey data, but custom scroll sequences build a cohesive product story. By structuring layout shifts to reveal materials block-by-block, we turn a simple specification sheet into a sensory brand interaction.
