07 / CASE STUDY

Udankhatola Ropeway

Simplifying ticket reservations, boarding logistics, and crowd navigation for thousands of tourists across spiritual destinations.

ROLE

UI/UX Lead Designer

TIMELINE

6 Weeks (2025)

DELIVERABLES

Mobile App UI, Boarding Dashboard

LOCATION

Gujarat, India

The Challenge

Ropeway sites (like Girnar or Pavagadh) experience massive daily tourist crowds. Long waiting lines, physical ticketing friction, and lack of real-time queue data create immense passenger stress.

We designed the Udankhatola mobile boarding companion. The interface addresses physical queue bottlenecks by introducing dynamic digital tickets, active time-slot reservations, and real-time status tracking so passengers can enjoy local sights instead of standing in line.

DESIGN CRITERIA

Designing for highly diverse user demographics, requiring extremely high legibility, multilingual options, and offline-compatible QR codes.

User Flow & Crowds

Inclusive Layouts

Ropeway passengers span all age groups, including elderly pilgrims. We prioritized high-contrast layouts, large buttons, and direct icons. The app uses dynamic boarding statuses (e.g. "Your Boarding Group: C-4", "Estimated Wait: 15 mins") to keep information simple and glanceable.

Boarding companion flow

1. Select Destination — Choose the ropeway facility.
2. Time Block Booking — Secure a boarding slot (e.g., 9:00 AM - 10:00 AM).
3. Offline QR Receipt — Ticket works even with weak internet at high altitudes.
4. Queue Alerts — Receive notifications when your group is called to board.

Validated Impact

  • Estimated 50% drop in queue line crowd congestion via dynamic grouping.
  • QR boarding validation takes under 2 seconds at turnstiles.
  • Highly praised UI simplicity by senior pilgrims during field tests.

Service Design Lesson

UX goes beyond screen interfaces. In public utilities, the digital app is just one touchpoint in a larger physical ecosystem. By matching digital boarding groups with physical gate calling lights, we created a unified system that successfully navigates diverse crowds through high-density choke points.
