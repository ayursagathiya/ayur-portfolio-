01 / CASE STUDY

Voltify EV Charging

Designing a seamless smart charging experience for electric vehicles, addressing range anxiety with real-time feedback loops.

ROLE

UI/UX Lead Designer

TIMELINE

6 Weeks (2025)

DELIVERABLES

iOS Mobile App, Design System

LOCATION

Ahmedabad, India

The Challenge

EV drivers suffer from acute "range anxiety" — the constant fear that their battery will drain before reaching a charging station.

Through preliminary surveys, we identified that range anxiety is aggravated by inaccurate app data: drivers arrive at charging ports only to discover they are broken, occupied, or incompatible with their vehicle plug type.

CORE QUESTION

How might we design a frictionless charging station reservation and status monitoring app that provides reassuring micro-interactions and transparent real-time state displays?

User Research & Persona

PERSONA PROFILE

Vikram Shah, 34

Role: Sales Executive traveling daily between Ahmedabad and Vadodara (120km).

Frictions: Arriving at chargers that have long queues, leading to missed client appointments. Needs absolute confidence in charger status before booking.

USER JOURNEY MAP
  • Step 1: Check Range — Vikram notices 18% remaining battery (Triggers range anxiety).
  • Step 2: Find Charger — Opens Voltify, filters by "Fast DC Charger" compatible with his plug type.
  • Step 3: Reserve Port — reserves Ahmedabad Station port A-4 for 30 minutes. Status updates instantly.
  • Step 4: Charging Loop — Plugs in and receives live, responsive percentage updates and cost calculation.

Project Results

  • 48% reduction in booking cancellation rates due to precise live sensor tracking.
  • 9.2/10 rating on User Ease Questionnaire due to simple circular battery visual metrics.
  • Successful prototype validation across a pilot fleet testing pool in Ahmedabad.

Lessons Learned

EV charging design requires extreme information hierarchy prioritization. When a driver is navigating at night, complex tables fail. Replacing data charts with a single circular glow gauge and bright semantic color codes (e.g. green for charging) drastically improves cognitive clarity and navigation safety.
